FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Honest answers — no marketing spin.

Does Runday actually send emails on my behalf, or just draft them?

Both — your choice. By default, Runday drafts messages for your approval. You can configure specific workflows (like invoice reminders) to send automatically, or keep full control and just review drafts before they go out.

How secure is connecting my accounts?

We use OAuth — the same secure login standard used by major platforms — so we never see your passwords. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and you can disconnect any integration at any time from your settings.

What if my business is a bit unusual — will Runday understand how I work?

Runday is configured through plain English. During setup you describe your follow-up rules, tone, scheduling preferences, and business context. The more you tell it, the better it fits your workflow. Early-access customers also get direct access to our team to dial things in.

I'm not technical. Will setup actually be easy?

Yes. There are no APIs to configure, no Zapier-style workflows to build, and no spreadsheets to maintain. You connect accounts with standard logins and describe your business in plain text. Most users are up and running in under ten minutes.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. There are no contracts or lock-in periods. Cancel from your account settings at any time and you won't be charged again. We'd rather you stay because Runday genuinely helps you than because leaving feels like a hassle.

Is this right for my industry?

Runday is built specifically for service-based small businesses — contractors, consultants, agencies, health & wellness practitioners, and creative professionals. If you have clients, invoices, a calendar, and leads, Runday is built for you.
